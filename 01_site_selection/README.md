# Site Selection

This stage identifies a suitable surface-accessible binding site on F-actin for probe design.

The selected region was evaluated to minimize overlap with known actin-binding interfaces and commonly used F-actin probes, while maintaining sufficient surface accessibility for docking the azobenzene photoswitch.

## Selection Criteria

* Surface accessibility
* Minimal overlap with known actin-binding proteins and probes
* Suitable local geometry for small-molecule docking
* Favorable physicochemical environment
* Compatibility with further peptide scaffold growth

## Selected Site

The final design workflow focused on the **patch212** region of F-actin.

This site was subsequently used for azobenzene docking and probe construction.
